Responsible AI Governance Policy

This policy defines risk-based AI governance for the assessed organization. AI use cases must be classified by autonomy, data sensitivity, user impact, regulatory exposure, and reversibility before production use.

Low-risk internal assistants may be approved by service-area owners and security reviewers. High-risk or autonomous scenarios require legal, security, data protection, and responsible AI review. Exceptions are time-bound, auditable, and linked to a named accountable owner.

Human oversight, disclosure, logging, escalation, fallback, and appeal requirements are tied to the risk class. Production release is blocked unless minimum controls are proven for the selected risk tier.
