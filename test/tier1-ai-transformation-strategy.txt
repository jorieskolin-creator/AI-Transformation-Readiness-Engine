AI Transformation Strategy

The strategy states that AI readiness is the organization ability to sense, decide, design, deliver, govern, learn, and scale AI-enabled value creation safely. AI is positioned as an integrated component of business architecture, not a standalone tool rollout.

The strategy prioritizes customer-service redesign, knowledge-work quality, decision support, and service-area productivity. It states that AI should not be used where accountability, data rights, safety, or human appeal cannot be proven.

The document is aspirational. It defines principles and ambition areas but does not prove operating cadence, production lifecycle controls, or measured adoption.
