Evidence-Gated Scanner Architecture

AI Transformation Readiness Engine

AI readiness is not technical readiness. This engine evaluates whether an organization can sense, decide, design, deliver, govern, learn, and scale AI-enabled value creation safely.

Reusable Engine Logic

The application keeps the proven scanner mechanics: dual-stream scoring, independent evidence verification, targeted rescans, deterministic metrics, confidence-gated synthesis, fact-checking, strategy sanitation, and GO/WARN/BLOCK quality gating.

Stream A
Maturity indicators

Evidence of operating, architectural, data, governance, and service capability readiness.

Stream B
Anti-patterns

Evidence of AI decision fog, pilot purgatory, brittle platform patterns, data context loss, and detached use-case logic.

Evidence Gate
Proof before score

Plans and slogans score low; operating proof scores higher; measured feedback loops score highest.

Readiness Ladder

  • Insufficient evidence
  • Emerging
  • Structured
  • Scaling with friction
  • Adaptive / Value-creating

Quality Contract

The engine may recommend action only inside the confidence allowed by verified evidence. Anti-pattern absence is credited only when it has been meaningfully tested, and roadmap tactics must map to locked findings or to the approved tactics database.
